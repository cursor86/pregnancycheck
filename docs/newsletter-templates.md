# Weekly Pregnancy Newsletter — 40 Ready-to-Paste Templates

Paste each week's **Subject** and **Body** into its own step of your ConvertKit
sequence, in order (Week 1 = Step 1, Week 2 = Step 2, ...). Set each step's
delay to trigger relative to the subscriber's `due_date` custom field so the
right email lands in the right week — see setup steps below.

Educational content only, not medical advice — each email should keep a
one-line reminder of that (already included at the bottom of the body).

## ConvertKit setup (one-time)

1. **Create account** → convertkit.com (free up to 10,000 subscribers)
2. **Add a custom field**: Subscribers → Custom Fields → New → name it `due_date`, type Date
3. **Create a form**: Grow → Landing Pages & Forms → New Form → add two fields: Email and `due_date`. Copy the Form ID (in the embed code / form settings URL) and the API Key (Account Settings → Developer)
4. **Create a sequence**: Automations → Sequences → New Sequence, name it "Weekly Pregnancy Updates"
5. For each of the 40 templates below, add a sequence step, paste the subject + body, and set its delay so it fires when `(today − due_date) ≈ (week − 40) weeks` — ConvertKit's "Automations" (not Sequences) let you trigger off a date field directly: Automations → New Automation → Trigger: "Field: due_date" → for each week, add an email action with a time offset from that date.
6. Send me the API key + form ID once this exists and I'll wire the site's signup form to POST straight to it.

## Week 1

**Subject:** Week 1: Welcome — here's how pregnancy counting actually works

**Body:**

> **Baby's Development**
> This may feel odd, but week 1 is counted from the first day of your last period — before conception has even happened. Doctors use this date because it's easier to pinpoint than ovulation.
>
> **Your Body**
> You may be having your period this week, or getting ready to. Your body is preparing an egg for release later in your cycle.
>
> **Tip for the Week**
> Start (or continue) a prenatal vitamin with folic acid — it's most protective in these very early weeks.
>
> **Countdown**
> 39 weeks to go (estimated).
>
> _This is educational content, not medical advice. Always confirm with your healthcare provider._

## Week 4

**Subject:** Week 4: Implantation — hello, poppy seed

**Body:**

> **Baby's Development**

>
> **Your Body**
> This is often the week a missed period first tips you off. Home pregnancy tests can usually detect hCG starting around now.
>
> **Tip for the Week**
> If your period is late, this is the week a home test is likely to turn positive if you're pregnant.

> **Countdown**
